What learned

A small map from sensors to actuators, one per motivational state, trained only by whether drive pressure fell afterwards (REINFORCE with an eligibility trace). Time on the dock goes from 6% in the first half hour to 36%, then over 90% from 60 minutes on. It learned to get there and stay. It did not learn to leave when full: it sits on the dock at 87% charge, and the gaps in the docked strip are short slips off the contacts.

What it can sense

Battery voltage (with real LiPo sag under load), a microphone that hears its own steppers and the station's clicks, two light sensors on a panning head, a bumper, IR distance ahead, and the station's Wi-Fi signal strength, refreshed every 5 s.

How typical this is

This is one of the better first lives. Across 8 organisms, learned reflexes lift mean lifespan from 1.26 h to about 1.9 h, and the skill is not yet stable across repeated lives. The numbers are in the simulator README.
